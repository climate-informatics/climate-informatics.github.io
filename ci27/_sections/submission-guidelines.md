## Submission guidelines {#submission-guidelines}

Full papers and extended abstracts follow the same submission and review process. See the
[Call for papers](#call-for-papers) for formats and page limits.

### Deadline

Full papers and extended abstracts are due on **5 February 2027** (23:59 AoE). Supplementary
material for full papers must be submitted by the same date.

### Submission system

Submissions are handled through Microsoft CMT (Conference Management Toolkit). Make sure you
submit to the correct track and select the paper category that best fits your work.

- Create a CMT account before submitting:
  [how to create an account](https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/docs/help/general/account-creation.html).
- Read the [instructions for authors](https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/docs/help/author/author-submission-form.html)
  on how to fill in the submission form.
- Submission portal: {% if page.ci27.submission_url != "" %}[CI 2027 on CMT]({{ page.ci27.submission_url }}){% else %}link available from {{ page.ci27.submission_opens }}{% endif %}.

### Anonymization

Reviewing is double-blind, so the initial submission must not include author names or
affiliations. Remove or rephrase anything that could identify the authors, such as
acknowledgements, self-citations and grant numbers. These can be restored in the camera-ready
version after acceptance.

### Originality and reuse of text

- Submissions must present new, original work that has not been published elsewhere and is not
  under review at another venue at the same time. A paper counts as published if it appeared
  in an archived, peer-reviewed venue that people who did not attend can access.
- Concurrent submissions will be rejected without review.
- Reusing previously published text verbatim is not allowed. Previously published images may
  be reused only if they are cited and the necessary rights have been obtained.
- Allowed exceptions: preprints on non-archival repositories such as arXiv, citations of
  theses, and new versions of earlier non-archival work (such as posters or workshop papers)
  that add substantial new material and cite the earlier version.

### Submission limit {#submission-limit}

Each participant may submit only one manuscript (full paper or extended abstract) as submitting
author. Researchers may be listed as co-authors on more than one manuscript, but each additional
manuscript must be submitted, and if accepted presented, by a different co-author. If the same
person submits more than one manuscript, only the most recently submitted one will be considered
for review; all earlier submissions will be desk-rejected.

### Supplementary material

Supplementary material such as code, data, evaluation protocols, additional results and
figures is encouraged. Report the key parameters of your algorithms, use open data where
possible and, if you rely on proprietary or external data, share a version of it if you can.
The main submission must stand on its own: reviewers may look at supplementary material but
are not required to.

### Use of generative AI {#use-of-generative-ai}

Authors may use generative AI tools when preparing their manuscripts. Any such use must be
explicitly disclosed in a dedicated section titled "Declaration of Generative AI Use", placed
before the references. This section should name the tool(s) used and briefly describe their
purpose (e.g., language editing, code generation, figure preparation). AI tools cannot be
listed as authors, and the human authors remain fully responsible for the content, including
avoiding plagiarism, misrepresentation and falsification. If no generative AI tools were used,
authors should state this explicitly.

### Open science

Authors are encouraged to follow open-science practices and to accompany their submission with
a project repository (for example on GitHub). [The Turing Way](https://book.the-turing-way.org/)
explains how to set up a repository, choose a licence and obtain a DOI. Authors of full papers
should also follow the Cambridge University Press Research Transparency Policy on sharing data
and code.
