## Author guidelines {#author-guidelines}

Climate Informatics welcomes high-quality, novel contributions that bridge climate and weather
research with data science and advance data-driven methods in the atmospheric and climate
sciences. Submissions can be **full papers** or **extended abstracts**; see the
[Call for papers](#call-for-papers) and the [Submission guidelines](#submission-guidelines)
for details.

### Formatting requirements

- Full papers use the *Environmental Data Science* templates; extended abstracts use the
  CI 2027 template ([Word]({{ page.ci27.template_word | relative_url }}) ·
  [LaTeX]({{ page.ci27.template_latex | relative_url }})).
- All files, including supplementary material, must be in **PDF** format and written in
  **English**.
- Figures and tables must be readable both in colour and in greyscale. Colour-blind-safe
  palettes are recommended for all coloured figures.

### Submission requirements

- Submissions are made through the conference CMT portal. Submission portal: {% if page.ci27.submission_url != "" %}[CI 2027 on CMT]({{ page.ci27.submission_url }}){% else %}link available
  from {{ page.ci27.submission_opens }}{% endif %}.
- Each participant may submit only **one manuscript** as submitting author
  (see [Submission limit](#submission-limit)).
- Every manuscript must include a "Declaration of Generative AI Use" section before the
  references (see [Use of generative AI](#use-of-generative-ai)).
- The corresponding author must make sure that all co-authors approve the results presented
  and the final manuscript.

### Review and publication

- Each submission is reviewed double-blind by at least two independent reviewers.
- Remove all identifying information (names, affiliations, acknowledgements) before
  submitting: submissions that are not anonymized will be desk-rejected.
- Accepted full papers that are presented at the conference will be published in a special
  collection of *Environmental Data Science*. Instructions for this step will be sent after
  the conference.

### Registration and attendance

CI 2027 is an in-person event; remote presentations will not be possible. Each accepted paper
or extended abstract must be presented at the conference by its submitting author or by another
co-author who has not submitted a different manuscript. A separate registration fee must be paid
for each accepted contribution; one registration cannot cover more than one paper. Contributions
that are not registered by the registration deadline will be withdrawn from the programme and
will not be included in the proceedings.

### Good practice

- Climate Informatics brings together climate scientists, meteorologists, data scientists and
  policy researchers: write for an interdisciplinary audience.
- Follow reproducible-research practices: include a data availability statement, links to
  code repositories and, where possible, to open datasets.
- Authors from under-represented backgrounds and regions are strongly encouraged to submit.

For questions about submissions or formatting, write to the organizing committee
(see [Contact](#contact)).
